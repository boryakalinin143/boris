01 + 02 · TRUE TO THE LOT, MEASURED

We roast for the lot. We name it by a number.

There is no house style at SUR. Every lot gets the profile that brings out its best: a dense Ethiopian takes a long, gentle roast, a soft Brazilian a short one. Both reach the same measured colour, Agtron 075. One number, one roast, every time.

1 M Kč, in years 3 and 4.

2 · FIVE YEARS OF DIVIDENDS

Then five years of dividends: 988 000 Kč.

Once the loan is repaid, investors get their 1 % of SUR’s full net income every year. The founders take only half of theirs and leave the rest in the company. Your ticket brings 155 000 Kč in year 5, growing to 237 000 Kč in year 9.
